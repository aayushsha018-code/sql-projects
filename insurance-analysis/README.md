# Insurance SQL Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes an insurance dataset using SQL to identify key factors affecting medical charges and derive actionable business insights.

---

## 📊 Objectives
- Analyze factors influencing insurance charges  
- Compare charges across different customer segments  
- Identify high-risk and high-cost customers  
- Understand the impact of age, BMI, smoking, and region  

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- MySQL  
- SQL (joins, aggregations, subqueries, window functions)  

---

## 📁 Dataset
The dataset includes:
- customer demographics (age, gender)  
- bmi (body mass index)  
- number of children  
- smoking status  
- region  
- insurance charges  

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis Performed
- compared charges between smokers and non-smokers  
- analyzed impact of bmi on medical costs  
- evaluated regional differences in insurance charges  
- segmented customers based on age groups and risk levels  
- identified top customers with highest charges  

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- smokers have significantly higher charges than non-smokers  
- higher bmi is associated with increased medical costs  
- charges tend to increase with age  
- some regions have consistently higher insurance costs  

---

## 🚀 Recommendations
- design targeted plans for high-risk customers  
- promote health awareness programs to reduce risk factors  
- optimize pricing strategies based on customer segments  
- focus on preventive care initiatives  

---

## 📂 Files
- `insurance_project.sql` → contains all SQL queries, analysis, insights, and recommendations
